Budget Builder Quest

Build a monthly budget, live with it for 5 months of real-life curveballs, and see how your choices impact your Money Health Score.

About

Budget Builder Quest is a free money game from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. Pick a life, build a monthly budget, then live with it for 5 months of real-life curveballs and see your Money Health Score.

How it works

  • Step 1: Choose a life to practice with.
  • Step 2: Build your monthly budget. You can’t spend more than you earn.
  • Step 3: Handle a money curveball each month and watch your checking, emergency fund, and credit card.

The amounts are simplified examples for learning. This is not financial advice.
