# Privacy Policy — Budget Builder Quest

Budget Builder Quest is an offline-first educational Android app from WGRALGO /
The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

- No account is required.
- No personal information is requested.
- No analytics, telemetry, or tracking SDKs are bundled.
- No advertising identifiers are read.
- No crash reporters phone home.

## Network access

The Android app declares **no** `INTERNET` permission. It cannot connect to any
server, even if instructed to. All gameplay runs locally inside the on-device
WebView shell.

## Data you enter

The income amounts, category allocations, and choices you enter during a Budget
Builder Quest round are **simulation inputs only**.

- They live in your device's memory while you play.
- They are discarded when the app process ends.
- They are never written to disk, uploaded, or shared with WGRALGO.
- The app does not request file-system, contacts, location, microphone, or
  camera permissions.

If you choose to take a screenshot and share it yourself, that is your
decision. The app does not initiate any sharing.

## No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions

The app has zero monetization surfaces. There are no ads, no paid tiers, no
subscriptions, no donation prompts inside the APK, and no upsells.

## Children

Budget Builder Quest is designed as an educational tool that is appropriate for
all ages. It does not collect any data from any user, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If the privacy posture of the app ever changes in a future release, this file
will be updated in the public repository before that release is published.
